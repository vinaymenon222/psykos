🎬 5-minute progressive muscle relaxation

Sit or lie down comfortably. For each body part, tighten the muscles for 5 seconds (not to the point of pain), then let go for 15 seconds and notice the difference. Skip any area that is injured or sore. Turn on your sound for the voice and chimes.
