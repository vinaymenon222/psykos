English for engineers

Subject-verb agreement

A verb must match its subject in number. Reports, lab sheets and project documents all depend on it. Learn eleven rules, from the basic to the tricky, and then take a test that adapts to your answers.

  1. 1Read the rules in order. Each has three examples from science and engineering.
  2. 2Do the five-sentence practice under each rule. Aim for 5 out of 5 before moving on.
  3. 3Take the 25-statement test. Right answers make the next one harder, wrong answers make it easier.
  4. 4Read your remarks to see exactly which rules to learn again.
